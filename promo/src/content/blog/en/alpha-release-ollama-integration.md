# Timeline Studio Alpha v0.60.0: Professional Video Editor with Free Local AI

*August 3, 2024*

We're excited to introduce the first public alpha release of Timeline Studio — a professional video editor with a revolutionary approach to AI: **everything works locally and for free!**

## 🚀 Main Feature: 100% Local AI

While other video editors require expensive cloud AI service subscriptions, Timeline Studio works with **free local AI models**:

- **No subscriptions** — use AI as much as you want for free
- **Complete privacy** — your data stays on your machine
- **Offline work** — no internet needed after downloading models
- **Model choice** — Llama, Phi, Gemma, Qwen and more via local inference

### What AI can do in alpha version:

- 📝 **Subtitle generation** — automatic transcription and synchronization
- 🎬 **Video analysis** — scene and key moment detection
- 🎯 **Smart hints** — AI helps with editing
- 🔍 **Content recognition** — frame composition and quality analysis

## 💎 Full-Featured Video Editor for FREE

Timeline Studio isn't just "another editor". Even in alpha version you get:

### Core features:
- ✅ **Multi-track timeline** — unlimited video and audio tracks
- ✅ **Popular formats** — MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV and more
- ✅ **Transitions and effects** — basic set for professional work
- ✅ **Full HD export** — without watermarks!
- ✅ **GPU acceleration** — fast rendering on supported video cards

### Unique features:
- 🌍 **15 interface languages** — including Russian, Chinese, Japanese
- ⚡ **Lightning performance** — thanks to Tauri v2 and optimizations
- 🔒 **Maximum security** — process isolation, XSS protection
- 💾 **Local storage** — all data stays on your computer

## 🎯 Our Philosophy: "Free Locally, Paid in Cloud"

We believe that basic editing tools and AI should be accessible to everyone:

- **Local = Free**: Install local AI runtime, choose models, work without limits
- **Cloud = Convenience**: For those who prefer not to configure anything

## 📦 How to Start

### 1. Install Local AI Runtime (5 minutes)
Choose one of the supported options:
- Ollama (easiest for beginners)
- LM Studio
- llama.cpp
- Other compatible local inference tools

### 2. Download AI model (3-5 minutes)
Recommended models:
- Llama 3.2 (balanced performance)
- Phi-3 (lightweight)
- Gemma 2 (Google)
- Qwen 2.5 (multilingual)

### 3. Launch Timeline Studio
Just open the app — it will automatically detect your local AI setup and available models!
