# Master AI Features in Timeline Studio

Timeline Studio comes with 257 AI-powered features designed to accelerate your workflow. This guide will help you get the most out of these powerful tools.

## Getting Started with AI

### Setting Up AI Providers

Timeline Studio supports multiple AI providers:

1. **Claude (Anthropic)** - Best for creative content analysis
2. **OpenAI GPT-4** - Excellent for script generation
3. **DeepSeek** - Fast and cost-effective
4. **Ollama (Local)** - Privacy-focused, runs on your machine

Configure your preferred provider in Settings → AI Services.

## Essential AI Features

### Smart Montage Planning

Let AI analyze your footage and create optimized cut sequences:

1. Select your clips in the browser
2. Click "AI Montage Planner"
3. Set your target duration and style
4. Review and apply suggestions

### Auto Scene Detection

AI automatically identifies scene changes, making it easy to organize long recordings:

- Real-time detection during import
- Manual refinement with AI assistance
- Export scene markers for other tools

### Content-Aware Editing

AI understands your content and suggests:
- Best moments to highlight
- Optimal trim points
- Transition recommendations
- Background music that matches the mood

## Advanced Techniques

### Multi-Platform Optimization

Use AI to automatically adapt content for different platforms:

```
1. Complete your edit
2. Select "Multi-Platform Export"
3. AI will suggest platform-specific adjustments
4. Export all versions at once
```

### Subtitle Generation

Generate accurate subtitles in 15 languages:
- Automatic speech recognition
- Speaker identification
- Timing optimization
- Style customization

## Tips & Tricks

- Use AI suggestions as a starting point, not the final word
- Combine multiple AI providers for best results
- Train custom AI models with your editing style
- Leverage local AI (Ollama) for sensitive content

## Performance Optimization

AI features work best with:
- At least 16GB RAM
- Modern GPU (GTX 1660 or better)
- SSD storage for video files
- Stable internet for cloud AI providers

Start experimenting with these features today and see how AI can transform your editing workflow!
